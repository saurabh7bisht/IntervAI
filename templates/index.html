Step-by-Step Flow

How IntervAI Works

From candidate onboarding to AI-driven evaluation in four simple steps.

STEP 01
Configure Track

Select your target role, experience level (Student to Executive), interview mode (Real vs. Practice), and choose between 5 to 12 questions.

STEP 02
Upload Resume & JD

Upload your PDF/Word resume and the employer's Job Description. Our extraction engine securely verifies and reads both documents in real time.

STEP 03
AI Gap Scanning

The LLM cross-references the JD against your background, isolates missing skills, and formulates a progressive question roadmap tailored to you.

STEP 04
Live Session & Report

Interact via voice or text, manage your timed countdown or practice hints, then receive instant radar analytics, sub-scores, and improvement tips.
